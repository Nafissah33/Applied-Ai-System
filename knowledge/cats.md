## Adult cat daily routine
Adult cats need regular meals, fresh water, a clean litter box, and play to stay active. Scoop the litter box at least once a day.
TASK: Morning feeding | 5 | high | feeding | 08:00
TASK: Fresh water check | 5 | high | feeding |
TASK: Litter box scooping | 5 | high | litter |
TASK: Interactive play session | 15 | medium | play |
TASK: Evening feeding | 5 | high | feeding | 18:00

## Kitten care
Kittens need several small meals a day, lots of short play sessions, and gentle handling and socialization. Keep the litter box clean and easy to reach.
TASK: Kitten meal | 5 | high | feeding | 08:00
TASK: Kitten play session | 10 | medium | play |
TASK: Gentle handling and socialization | 10 | medium | play |

## Senior cat care
Senior cats need easy access to the litter box, soft warm resting spots, and gentle play. Watch appetite, thirst and mobility closely.
TASK: Gentle play | 10 | medium | play |
TASK: Appetite and water check | 5 | high | feeding |
TASK: Soft bedding check | 5 | low | grooming |

## Indoor cat enrichment
Indoor cats need climbing spaces, scratching posts, window perches and rotating toys to prevent boredom.
TASK: Enrichment toy rotation | 10 | medium | play |
TASK: Climbing and scratching time | 10 | medium | play |

## Cat grooming
Brush cats weekly, more for long hair, and check teeth and claws.
TASK: Brushing | 10 | low | grooming |
TASK: Claw trim | 5 | low | grooming |
