## Adult dog daily routine
Adult dogs do best with two meals a day and at least 30 to 60 minutes of exercise. Keep feeding times consistent and always leave fresh water out.
TASK: Morning feeding | 10 | high | feeding | 08:00
TASK: Fresh water check | 5 | high | feeding |
TASK: Daily walk | 30 | high | walk |
TASK: Evening feeding | 10 | high | feeding | 18:00

## Puppy care
Puppies need three or four small meals a day, short play sessions, frequent potty breaks, and early training and socialization. Sessions should be brief because puppies tire quickly.
TASK: Puppy meal | 10 | high | feeding | 08:00
TASK: Potty break | 5 | high | walk |
TASK: Short training session | 10 | medium | play |
TASK: Socialization play | 15 | medium | play |

## Senior dog care
Senior dogs need shorter, gentler walks and warm comfortable bedding. Watch for stiffness after rest. Keep a regular schedule and check mobility daily.
TASK: Gentle short walk | 15 | high | walk |
TASK: Mobility check | 5 | medium | grooming |
TASK: Warm bedding check | 5 | low | grooming |

## Joint and arthritis support for dogs
Dogs with joint pain or arthritis benefit from low-impact exercise such as slow leash walks and avoiding stairs or jumping. Gentle massage and warm bedding help. Ask a vet about any supplements or treatment.
TASK: Low-impact leash walk | 15 | high | walk |
TASK: Gentle joint massage | 10 | medium | grooming |

## Weight management for dogs
Overweight dogs need measured portions, fewer treats, and extra daily activity. Weigh food instead of estimating it.
TASK: Measured portion feeding | 10 | high | feeding |
TASK: Extra activity walk | 20 | medium | walk |

## Anxious or high-energy dogs
Anxious or energetic dogs need enrichment such as puzzle feeders, sniff walks, and calm quiet time to burn energy and reduce stress.
TASK: Puzzle feeder enrichment | 15 | medium | play |
TASK: Sniff walk | 20 | medium | walk |

## Dog grooming
Brush coats a few times a week. Check ears and teeth weekly and trim nails every few weeks.
TASK: Brushing | 10 | low | grooming |
TASK: Teeth and ear check | 5 | low | grooming |
