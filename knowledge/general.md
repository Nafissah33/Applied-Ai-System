## Essential daily care
Every pet needs food, fresh water, safe shelter and attention each day. Feeding and water are never optional.
TASK: Feeding | 10 | high | feeding |
TASK: Fresh water check | 5 | high | feeding |

## Time budgeting tips
When time is short, protect feeding, water and medication first, then exercise, then grooming and play. Never skip essentials to fit enrichment.

## Medication and health tasks
Medication reminders can be scheduled as tasks, but doses and treatments must come from a veterinarian. This system does not give dosage advice. Scheduling a daily medication reminder is fine when a vet has prescribed it.
TASK: Give prescribed medication (as directed by vet) | 5 | high | medication |

## Hot weather and safety
In hot weather walk early or late, bring water, and avoid hot pavement.
TASK: Cool-hours walk | 20 | medium | walk |
